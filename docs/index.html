Why Traceface is different

Most detectors are a bare CNN in a notebook. Traceface is a forensic workstation.

🧠

Deep-learning CNN

4-layer Keras convolutional network trained on real vs AI-generated imagery, outputting a calibrated P(real) score.

🔬

ELA forensics

Error Level Analysis recompresses the image and amplifies compression differences — edited or synthesized regions light up.

🏷️

AI-signature scan

Extracts EXIF & PNG metadata and hunts for generator fingerprints: Stable Diffusion, Midjourney, DALL-E, ComfyUI…

🔥

Grad-CAM explainability

Instead of a black-box label, see which pixels drove the verdict with red-hot attention heatmaps.

🎬

Video deepfake screening

Samples frames across the clip, scores each with the CNN, and fuses them temporally — with a suspicion timeline.

⚖️

Honest by design

Every verdict is probabilistic with stated limitations. Forensics are supporting signals — never fake certainty.

How it works

1

Upload

Drop an image (JPG/PNG) or a video (MP4/MOV/AVI/WEBM).

2

Analyze

The CNN scores it while ELA, metadata and Grad-CAM run in parallel. Videos are screened frame-by-frame.

3

Verdict

Get a Real / AI-Generated verdict with confidence, forensic evidence and an explainability heatmap.
